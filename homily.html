Catholic Artifact: The Homily

For 2 millenia now people have been pouring over the words of Jesus Christ and His disciples looking for answers to their modern (or perhaps not so modern) problems. This, naturally, has bred a lot of discussion surrounding and intepretation of the Bible. As it happens, when you ask three christians a question about what Jesus meant when He said this or that, you will often get four different answers. If Jesus would hurry up and come again so He could answer our questions, that would be great, but until the Rapture, we have to be content with having 2 to 3 billion different interpretations of the Bible.

With each interpretation comes a mode of sharing said interpretation. In the digital age this could include a tweet, a post on a forum, a video essay, or a podcast. There are also the more traditional modes by which an intepretation of the Bible is shared, like a sermon or a homily.
